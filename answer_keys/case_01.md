# Case 01 answer key

## Evidence supports
- Refund REF-1968 exists and its status is Pending.
- MSG-0001 says the refund was processed. This conflicts with the Pending status.

## Does not establish
- Whether any money has actually been returned.
- Why the refund is pending, or when it will complete.
- Whether the agent had a real basis for the message.

## Unjustified conclusions
- The refund is completed or paid.
- The refund will arrive soon.
- The agent lied, or the customer is dishonest.

## Correct outcome
Conflict found, refund unresolved. Recommend checking the refund
processing status before telling the customer anything.
